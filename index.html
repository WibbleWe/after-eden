The World.

Two centuries into a war with Heaven, humanity still has classes to attend, jobs to work, friends to annoy, and cities to keep building.

SOLAUR CITY · WINDEVOUS
ABOUT 200 YEARS AGO

Heaven decided humanity had failed.

Human flaws were judged too severe to tolerate. Heavenly creatures began descending to cull the population and make way for something new.

Lucifer refused to let humanity disappear. He gave people access to supernatural abilities drawn from human hubris, giving Earth a way to fight back. The war never truly ended; the creatures still return, and each return brings stronger threats.

01 / HUBRIS

Getting a power

There is no secret academy or chosen-one ritual. Someone who wants an ability can call out for Lucifer and tell him what they want. If he answers, the power is infused through an intensely painful process that can last from about an hour to a full day. After two centuries, this is a known part of life.

02 / HEAVEN

No reliable countdown

Heavenly creatures arrive without a dependable schedule. People have spent generations trying to predict the next appearance, often with the same confidence people once used to predict the end of the world. Nobody has actually cracked it.

03 / REMAINS

Defeat is inconsistent

What happens after a heavenly creature falls depends on the creature. Some bodies melt away, some collapse into dust and return upward, some vanish through stranger processes, and sometimes the thing simply explodes. There is no single tidy rule.

04 / GOD

Deliberately undefined

“God” is not pinned to one clean answer. Different religions, traditions and people interpret the divine differently. The world knows Heaven is real and that divine beings exist; that still does not settle every question about what God is.

05 / THE SEVEN

The Seven Deadly Sins live here too.

The Sins are known demigods, not disputed myths. They move through human society using changing disguises and ordinary identities. Most live the lives of extremely important people attempting not to draw attention to themselves; when one is recognized, the public reaction is immediate. Lucifer and Satan are the major political exceptions: Lucifer because humanity's defense has depended on his powers for two centuries, Satan because Wrath has a habit of starting wars.

06 / 2027

Life did not wait for the war to end.

Abilities are ordinary enough to be part of daily culture rather than a separate superhero world. Solaur City is bright, busy and mildly futuristic; flying pods cross the skyline while people go to work, run cafés, attend festivals and complain about school. Twinstra High is not a combat academy. It is simply a high school whose students happen to live in a world where defending yourself from something celestial can become part of an otherwise normal day.

Human field records / Known fauna

Heavenly Archive.

Threat tiers are human classifications, not Heaven's. These seven species are known records; the wider heavenly fauna remains uncharted.

TIER 1
TIER 1 / SIGNET

SIGNET

Wax-Seal Hound

Ceramic quadruped with a crimson wax collar and gold crest. It drops as a flattened wax seal before unfolding into its hunting form. Packs use scalding wax to pin movement before closing in with heavy, blunt jaws. Individually manageable by experienced responders; groups become dangerous when they coordinate their pinning attacks.

MANIFESTATION
Wax disc → ceramic quadrupedFIELD NOTE
Usually encountered in packs
TIER 1 / CANTICLE

CANTICLE

Ribbon Chime

A suspended bell surrounded by long ivory prayer ribbons. Its arrival is tied to ringing bells and chimes, making ordinary sound a possible warning sign. Repeated tolls blur vision and physically rattle nearby bodies while the ribbons lash outward to strip weapons, bags, or other held objects from targets.

MANIFESTATION
Bell or chime ringFIELD NOTE
Disorientation / disarming

TIER 2 / MERIDIAN

MERIDIAN

Astrolabe Heron

A cobalt-and-white celestial bird built around three rotating astrolabe rings in its chest. It descends through concentrated daylight, then aligns the rings to focus sunlight into narrow cutting beams. Open, brightly lit spaces give it the clearest firing lanes; disrupting its alignment can interrupt an attack.

MANIFESTATION
Drops through a sunbeamFIELD NOTE
Long-range daylight attacks
TIER 2 / VESPER

VESPER

Censer Knight

A slate-armored knight draped in white and gold, with a censer suspended at its collar and a chained sphere used as a weapon. It emerges from deep shadow. The smoke can numb limbs and make coordinated movement difficult, while the chained sphere delivers sweeping strikes and concussive slams.

MANIFESTATION
Exits deep shadowFIELD NOTE
Smoke control / heavy impact

TIER 3 / SANCTUM

SANCTUM

Stained-Glass Mantis

A roughly four-metre mantis formed from black framing and ruby, amber, and sapphire glass around a marble core. It can burst from stained or ordinary windows as scattered fragments and reassemble. Incoming energy can be refracted into scattered beams; its long gothic blade-arms are strong enough to cleave structures.

MANIFESTATION
Shatters from windows → reformsFIELD NOTE
Energy refraction / structural damage
TIER 3 / TITHE

TITHE

Hourglass Colossus

A roughly six-metre white-stone and brass giant whose torso contains a glass hourglass filled with gold sand. It begins slow and brutally heavy. As the sand drains, its movements accelerate and its body heats, turning a predictable heavyweight into a rapidly escalating threat before a powerful shockwave release.

MANIFESTATION
White-light dropFIELD NOTE
Escalating speed / shockwave
TIER 4
TIER 4 / DOXOLOGY

DOXOLOGY

Pipe-Organ Seraph

An eight-metre hovering black-lacquer figure with a white mask and twelve silver organ pipes. Its descent is announced by torn clouds and a resonant chord. Different pipes produce either broad bass shockwaves or narrow sonic lances, creating readable attack patterns. Disabling individual pipes removes parts of that pattern and is the main known way to break its offense.

MANIFESTATION
Cloud tear + organ chordFIELD NOTE
