The World.

SOLAUR CITY — DAYLIGHT
200 YEARS EARLIER

Heaven made a decision.

The gods judged humanity too imperfect to continue. Celestial creatures were sent to reduce Earth's population and clear the way for a replacement.

Lucifer refused to surrender a world he'd helped create. He gave humans supernatural abilities drawn from their own hubris. Humans survived. Heaven's creatures still return, stronger each time.

NOW · 2027

And life goes on.

Solaur City is bright, thriving, and mildly futuristic. Piloted pods cross the sky; everyday life carries on around the occasional invasion. Abilities are common, but not everyone uses them to fight.

Twinstra High is an ordinary school in an extraordinary city. Class B-5 has its own problems, friendships, and adventures.
